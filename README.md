# Fractional Operations Landing Page

**Live Site:** `yuexmenbot.github.io/fractional-ops-landing`

## Overview

This is a standalone landing page for Sebastian Mueller's fractional operations partnership service, targeting founders who are drowning in operational tasks they hate.

## Applied Skills

This landing page applies multiple acquired skills:

### 1. Direct Response Marketing
- **PAS Framework:** Problem (operations chaos) → Agitation (15+ hours weekly) → Solution (fractional partnership)
- **Specificity:** Exact hour savings, concrete pricing, specific outcomes
- **One reader approach:** Written to individual founder, not generic audience
- **Immediate action:** Clear CTAs with specific next steps

### 2. Pricing Psychology
- **Anchoring:** $150,000+ full-time hire positioned before $5-8K/month offering
- **Context control:** Comparison to failed hires, search costs, management overhead
- **Premium positioning:** Price signals expertise rather than commodity service
- **Risk reduction:** 30-day money-back guarantee reduces decision friction

### 3. Sales Closing
- **Assumptive language:** "When should we start?" instead of "Would you like to buy?"
- **Objection handling:** Comprehensive FAQ addressing security, team, industry concerns
- **Multiple closing attempts:** Several CTAs throughout page journey
- **Discovery focus:** Questions in email template to qualify prospects

### 4. Delegation Frameworks
- **Pain point identification:** Addresses founder's core delegation challenges
- **"No management needed" emphasis:** Solves biggest delegation failure point
- **Team enhancement approach:** Shows understanding of existing team dynamics
- **Outcome ownership:** Focuses on autonomous execution rather than task completion

### 5. Proposal Writing
- **Self-closing copy:** Page designed to pre-qualify and educate prospects
- **Structured presentation:** Month-by-month breakdown shows clear progression
- **Risk mitigation:** Addresses common concerns before they arise
- **Value framing:** Positions cost against outcomes rather than time

## Target ICP

- **Revenue Stage:** $500K - $5M ARR/revenue
- **Operations Pain:** Spending 15+ hours/week on ops they hate
- **Hiring History:** Previously hired ops people who quit or underperformed
- **Budget Reality:** Can't justify $150K+ Head of Operations hire
- **Management Aversion:** Want someone senior who doesn't need oversight
